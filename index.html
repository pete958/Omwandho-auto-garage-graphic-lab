About Omwandho

Omwandho Graphic & Auto Lab is a creative and automotive service brand based in Thika, Kiganjo.

We help businesses and individuals present themselves professionally through quality design, printing, branding and vehicle painting.

Whether you need a simple poster, a complete business branding package or a vehicle transformed with a professional paint job, our goal is simple: quality work that makes you proud.

Yes. WhatsApp is one of the easiest ways to send your requirements, photos and design ideas.

Yes. We can handle the design and printing process depending on your project.

About Omwandho

Omwandho Graphic & Auto Lab is a creative and automotive service brand based in Thika, Kiganjo.

We help businesses and individuals present themselves professionally through quality design, printing, branding and vehicle painting.

Whether you need a simple poster, complete business branding or a vehicle transformed with professional paint work, our goal is simple: quality work that makes you proud.
